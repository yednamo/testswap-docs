# Rules and points

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| 14 days of testing (tester) | +10 |
| Accepted feedback (tester) | +2 |
| One tester for your app (developer) | −12 |
| Lowest allowed balance | −180 |

- A test lasts 14 days; at most 2 may be missed. If the test started at 6 pm or later in the tester’s time zone,
  the first day is the next one.
- SDK mode: a test counts only if the app confirmed the installation through Google Play Integrity
  (installed from Google Play by this account, certified device). In SDK mode only the SDK checks in days;
  there is no manual check-in on the website for such tests.
- Feedback: at least 80 characters, from day 3; after day 14 there are 3 more days to write it. If there is no
  feedback by the end of day 17, the test is closed as not completed and no points are awarded.
- Feedback without the developer’s decision for 3 days is accepted automatically. Rejected feedback can be rewritten
  once; a second rejection is reviewed by a moderator, who can revoke the test and refund the points.
- At a time: up to 5 tests per tester, up to 3 open apps per developer.
- If a developer deletes their Yednamo account, their apps are closed and the running tests end without points;
  already completed tests and awarded points are kept.
- Points have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money.

## Not allowed

Several accounts per person, emulators and device farms, joining without installing, copied or empty feedback,
swapping “participation” without actually using apps. Violations cancel points and block the account.
