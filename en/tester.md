# For testers

## Sign in

Sign in at https://testswap.yednamo.com with Google (Yednamo ID). One account works across all Yednamo services.

## How to take part

1. Pick an app in the [catalog](https://testswap.yednamo.com/en/apps).
2. Press “Join the test” and confirm the rules.
3. Follow the steps on the test page:
   - **Google Group**: join the group with the same Google account you will install the app with.
   - **Email list**: wait until the developer adds your email in Play Console. The test starts on that day.
     If the developer does not grant access within 7 days, the participation is closed automatically with no penalty,
     and you can join another app.
   - Accept the testing invitation on Google Play.
   - Install the app. If the app is marked “With SDK”, install it **only via your personal link** from the test page:
     this is how the app confirms that you installed it. Open the link in Chrome on your phone, not inside a messenger,
     and do not download the app from the “Become a tester” page.
   - If the app is already installed but the test page shows no confirmation, press “I installed the app, link it”
     there, on the phone where the app is: the app opens and receives your tag.
4. Open the app every day for 14 days. Days are counted in the time zone you chose on the website when joining
   the test, not by the device’s clock. If the test started at 6 pm or later in that time zone, the first day is the next one.
   - **With SDK**: the day is checked in automatically when you open the app (actually open a screen, not when
     the process starts in the background). The app verifies the installation through Google Play Integrity: you need
     a certified Android device (Android 8.0 or newer) with Google Play; emulators and modified firmware will not pass.
     In SDK mode only the app checks in days: there is no manual check-in button on the website for such tests,
     and the test counts only after the installation is confirmed.
   - **Without SDK**: press “I opened the app today” on the test page.
   If the app is still not opened by the end of day 3 (no check-in at all), the participation closes by itself and
   the spot goes to another tester. We warn you by email on day 2.
5. From day 1, write feedback (at least 80 characters): what you liked, what you didn’t, what broke, what was unclear.
   If something is unclear from the start, that is already feedback; no need to wait.
6. After the test is completed, do not leave the Google testers group or opt out of the test on Google Play until
   the developer closes the test: Google only counts testers who stay in the test. We will email you when you can
   leave. You can uninstall the app meanwhile.

## When a test counts

- 14 days have passed and no more than 2 days were missed.
- Feedback is written. If there is no feedback on day 15, you have 3 more days to write it. If there is still
  no feedback by the end of day 17, the test is closed as not completed and no points are awarded.

- SDK mode: the app confirmed the installation through Google Play Integrity at least once.

A completed test earns **+10 points**, accepted feedback another **+2**.
If the developer enabled showing feedback, your accepted feedback with your name may appear on the public app page
and on the “Launched” showcase. Write feedback that can be shown.
If the developer does not respond to your feedback within 3 days, it is accepted automatically. If the developer
rejects your rewritten feedback too, a TestSwap moderator reviews it.

## Messages to the developer

The test page has a private chat with the developer: questions about installing, signing in, unclear parts of the app.
Messages appear instantly, without reloading the page. If you have not read a reply within 10 minutes, you get an
email (at most once an hour per conversation). You can write while the test is running and for 7 more days after it
ends; after that the conversation is read-only. One message is up to 2000 characters, at most 30 messages per hour.
Nobody sees contact details, only names. For abuse or spam there is a “Report” button: a moderator reviews the
conversation. The chat does not replace feedback: points are awarded only for feedback on the test page.

## Limits

- At most 5 tests at a time.
- You cannot rejoin the same app.
- You can leave a test at any time, but you will not get points for it.

## Reminders

If you haven’t opened the app today, you get an email after 7 pm your time. You can turn reminders off
for a single test or entirely in settings, and via the link in the email.
